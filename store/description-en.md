# Slide 15 — Store Submission Materials

## Short description (CrazyGames / GameDistribution / itch.io)

The classic sliding number puzzle with a neon twist. Tap a tile beside the
gap to push a whole row or column, restore the order 1 to N, and beat your
records on 3×3, 4×4 and 5×5 boards.

(= 185 characters, within the 200 limit)

## Details

- Genre: Puzzle / Casual / Classic board
- Controls: Mouse / Touch (tap any tile in the empty slot's row or column —
  the whole line slides one step). Keyboard: arrow keys move the tile next
  to the gap.
- Features: 3×3 / 4×4 / 5×5 grids, always-solvable shuffles (random legal
  moves from the solved board), multi-tile line pushes, smooth slide
  animation, move counter + timer, per-size best records (moves then time),
  win celebration with glow + particles, mute toggle, procedural graphics
  and sounds, single-file HTML5 (no external assets), works offline once
  loaded.
- Tech: Vanilla JavaScript, Canvas, WebAudio. No frameworks, no tracking.
